About

I'm a data scientist at Amazon, working on generative AI and NLP: multi-agent workflows, retrieval systems, and the evaluation frameworks that decide whether a model is ready for production. Most of what I do is the unglamorous half of that sentence, turning research code into services other teams can depend on.

Before Amazon I spent six years at Booz Allen Hamilton, building analytics and computer vision systems for the Air Force and teaching machine learning to Intelligence Community analysts. I studied industrial and systems engineering at Virginia Tech.
